# Apollo Setup Guide — email-first outreach for Campbell3, LLC

Apollo does three jobs in one place: (1) build a list of the right practices, (2) find/verify their
emails, (3) send a tracked email sequence with your sample attached. This guide sets it up for the
Practice Revenue Blueprint cold-email campaign.

> Honest expectation: Apollo is excellent for tech/B2B companies and **decent but uneven for small
> independent doctor's offices.** You'll get solid coverage on group practices and office managers, and
> patchier coverage on tiny solo offices. For the ones Apollo misses, grab the email from the practice
> website (free). Treat Apollo as the bulk tool, the website as the backstop.

---

## Step 1 — Account & credits (10 min)
1. Go to **apollo.io → Sign up** (use your Google account / denise4183@gmail.com).
2. Plans & what matters for you:
   - **Free** — a few hundred email credits/month, limited sequences. Fine to test.
   - **Basic / Professional (~$49–99/mo)** — more email credits, full sequences, email sending. This is
     what you'd want for a real campaign.
   - "**Credits**" are spent when you **reveal/verify an email** (and phone). Searching and viewing
     masked results is effectively free; revealing the email costs a credit.
3. You don't need the API or any code — use Apollo's **web app**. (The integration in our chat is
     optional; the web app is the simplest path for you.)

## Step 2 — Build your target list (15 min)
In Apollo, go to **Search → People**, and set these filters:
- **Job titles:** Owner, Physician, Practice Manager, Office Manager, Practice Administrator, Practice Owner
- **Location:** Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania (add a radius / nearby suburbs if you want more)
- **Industry / Keywords:** "Hospital & Health Care", or keyword "family medicine" / "internal medicine"
- **Employees:** 1–50 (keeps you on **independent** practices, not big systems)
- (Optional) **Company name** — paste names from your `pittsburgh-call-list.md` to target specific practices
- Click **Save Search** and **add results to a List** (e.g. "Pittsburgh PCP — Blueprint").

## Step 3 — Find & verify emails (uses credits)
1. In your list, filter **Email Status = Verified** (these deliver best; avoid "unverified"/"guessed").
2. Select the contacts you want and click **Access Email** / **Save** to reveal them (this spends credits).
3. Start small — reveal your **top 20–25** (the named-physician / office-manager contacts) before doing
   the whole metro. Check quality first.

## Step 4 — Connect your sending email (10 min)
- **Settings → Mailboxes → Connect** your Google account (denise4183@gmail.com), OR — better for volume —
  buy a **dedicated domain** (e.g. campbell3.com once you own it) and a Google Workspace mailbox, and warm
  it up for ~2 weeks before heavy sending.
- For your first handful of clients, your Gmail is fine **at low volume** (20–30/day).

## Step 5 — Build the sequence (15 min)
**Sequences → New Sequence.** Recreate the 3-touch email sequence from `cold-outreach-kit.md`:
- **Step 1 (Day 1):** the cold email (subject: "The 2025 Medicare change most primary care practices missed"), **attach `westbrook-family-medicine.pdf`**.
- **Step 2 (Day 4):** auto follow-up "bump."
- **Step 3 (Day 8):** the "breakup" email.
Use Apollo **variables** (`{{first_name}}`, `{{company}}`) so each one is personalized.
Set a **daily send limit of 25–30** to protect deliverability.

## Step 6 — Compliance (Apollo helps here)
- Apollo auto-adds an **unsubscribe link** and tracks opt-outs — that covers the CAN-SPAM opt-out rule.
- Add your **business mailing address** in the sequence footer (legally required on commercial email).
- Keep the subject honest (it is). Honor every unsubscribe automatically (Apollo does this).

## Step 7 — Launch & work the replies
- Turn the sequence on for your first 20–25 contacts. Watch **opens and replies** in Apollo.
- Any reply → stop the sequence for that person and **reply personally** to book the 15-minute intake.
- Pair it with the phone: a call to a practice that already got your email lands much better.

---

## What it costs you, realistically
- **Time:** ~1 hour to set up, then minutes a day to work replies.
- **Money:** the plan (~$49–99/mo) + credits for emails revealed. Starting with 20–25 contacts keeps
  credit use tiny while you validate the channel.
- **Reputation:** keep daily volume low (25–30) and personalized. One booked intake pays for months of Apollo.

## When to skip Apollo
If you only want to approach your **top 15–20 Pittsburgh practices**, it's faster and free to grab their
office email from each practice's website and send from Gmail in small batches. Apollo earns its keep when
you want to work the **whole metro (100+)** systematically.
