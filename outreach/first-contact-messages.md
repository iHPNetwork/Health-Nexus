# Five First-Contact Messages

**How to use this file:** Replace every `[bracketed]` placeholder with a real name and detail before
sending. Each message is short on purpose — 3–4 sentences, honest, specific to that person's world. The
hook in every one is the same true fact: *Medicare changed how it pays for care management in 2025, and
most practices don't know yet.* The ask is one trial Blueprint at the discounted rate ($750 for the first
five clients, in exchange for a testimonial).

**Discount framing:** "first five practices / for a testimonial / $750 instead of $1,500." Use it plainly.

**A note on the signature:** swap `Campbell3, LLC` and `[Your Name]` throughout. Keep your real
credentials in the sign-off — they are the reason these land.

> **Moonlighting guardrail (read once):** none of these touch DPP coverage, health-plan negotiation, or
> employer group sales — your ADA lane. This is physician-side practice revenue analysis only. Still, do
> **not** send to any practice you work with directly through the ADA grant. If a name on your list is an
> ADA-grant practice, cross it off.
