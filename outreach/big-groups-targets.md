## How to approach a group (vs. a solo practice)
- Ask for the **Practice Administrator / Operations Director / CFO** — not a physician. At a multi-site
  group, that person owns the billing-optimization decision.
- Lead with the multi-site angle: *"Across your locations, the 2025 APCM change is likely worth seven
  figures you're not capturing. I'd like to show your administrator a free sample analysis."*
- These groups may already run some CCM — that's fine; the Blueprint shows whether they're optimized and
  what in-house vs. vendor vs. hybrid nets them. (See the "We already do CCM / use a vendor" objections.)

## Reaching them by email/form (your no-cold-call channel)
1. Open each website's **Contact** page → paste a tightened version of the cold email (subject + body).
2. In the message, offer to email the sample: *"Reply with the best address and I'll send a sample
   Practice Revenue Blueprint."* (Contact forms often don't take attachments — get the email, then send.)
3. Log it; follow up by the same form or a call in 3–4 business days.

> Note: these sites block automated lookups, so their direct admin emails aren't publicly listed — the
> contact form (or one call asking "what's the administrator's email?") is the way in.
