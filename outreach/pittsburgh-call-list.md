# Pittsburgh Cold-Call List — independent primary care practices

Pulled from the CMS NPI Registry (public data), filtered to **independent** Family/Internal Medicine
practices. Large health-system locations (Allegheny Health Network — "Allegheny Clinic" / "Allegheny
Medical Practice Network", Allegheny General Hospital) were **excluded** — they're slow procurement with
no owner-level decision-maker, the wrong first target for cold outreach.

**This is a starter batch (NPI records beginning A–B). Ask me to pull more (C–Z, specific suburbs/ZIPs).**

> Verify ownership on the call — some independents have been acquired but kept their old practice NPI.
> The front desk will tell you fast ("are you part of AHN/UPMC, or independent?").
> Moonlighting guardrail: skip any practice you serve through the ADA grant.

## How to work this list
1. Start at the top (Priority A). Use the cold-call script in `cold-outreach-kit.md`.
2. Goal of each call: permission to **email the sample**, or a **15-minute intake** booking.
3. No answer → voicemail + cold email same day (attach `samples/westbrook-family-medicine.pdf`).
4. Log each: Practice · Date · Outcome · Next action. Stop after 3 touches with no response.
5. ~10 dials gets you 1–2 conversations. One booked intake is a good day.
