# Practice Lens — What You Get (plain language)

Practice Lens helps a primary care office collect the Medicare money it is allowed to bill for taking care
of patients between visits — money most offices miss. You do it yourself, with our tool. **You keep 100% of
what you collect.** There is no revenue share and no per-patient fee.

---

## Free — "Your Number"
- You answer five simple questions about your practice.
- We instantly show you about how much Medicare money you are leaving uncollected each year.
- That is it. Free, three minutes, no obligation.

Think of it as a free check-up for your billing.

---

## $199 a month — "Starter: one program, run by you"
You pick **one** Medicare program to start with — for example, the yearly **Medicare Wellness Visit**, *or*
the monthly **care-management program (APCM)**. For that one program you get:

1. **Plain-English instructions** — who qualifies, and exactly what to do, step by step.
2. **Scripts your staff can read out loud** — how to invite a patient, how to get their okay, what to say each month.
3. **Fill-in templates** — the patient consent wording and a monthly checklist.
4. **A billing cheat-sheet** — which code to bill, how much it pays, and what to write in the chart so the claim is not denied.
5. **A simple tracker** — you type in how many patients you signed up and billed; it shows "you've collected
   $X of the $Y you could," and reminds you who is coming due.
6. **Always up to date** — when Medicare changes the rules or the payment amounts, the tool updates, so you
   are never billing the wrong thing.

**Best for:** a solo doctor or a small office starting with one program.

Everything in Starter, but for **all three money-makers**, not just one:

Plus a tracker that works for **several doctors** in the same practice.

Everything in Full, set up across **many offices**, with one combined view.

**Best for:** a group that owns several practices.

---

## Why it is a monthly price (the honest answer)
You pay monthly for two ongoing things:
1. **The tracker you actually use every month** to run the programs and see what you've collected.
2. **Keeping the Medicare rules and payment amounts current for you** — they change every year.

If you would rather **pay once instead of monthly**, we can offer a one-time setup price instead. Either
model works — it is a choice to make, not a rule.

---

## What Practice Lens is NOT (so there is no confusion)
- **It is not a billing company.** You (or your biller) still submit the claims; we tell you exactly what to submit.
- **It does not touch your patient records** and does not need a login to your health-record system.
- **It does not take a cut of your money.** You keep all of it.
- **It is not the same as the full report.** The one-time, expert-reviewed report (with the "$50k or you
  don't pay" guarantee) is a separate, deeper service. Practice Lens is the do-it-yourself tool.
